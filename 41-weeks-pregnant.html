👶 Baby's Development

Baby continues to grow. Placental function is monitored closely. Baby is considered overdue (post-term) this week.

🤰 What You May Feel

Induction is typically offered between 41 and 42 weeks. CTG monitoring checks baby's heart rate and movements regularly.

💡This week's tip: Induction is a safe, well-evidenced option. Discuss it with your doctor or healthcare provider — it significantly reduces risk beyond 42 weeks.
