Ultimate Pregnancy
Week-by-Week Checklist

A comprehensive, medically-reviewed checklist for every stage of your pregnancy to ensure you never miss a milestone or appointment.

First Trimester (Weeks 1-13)

Week 4: Take a home pregnancy test and schedule your first prenatal appointment (usually set for week 8).
Week 5: Start taking a daily prenatal vitamin containing at least 400mcg of folic acid.
Week 6: Adjust your diet: avoid raw fish, undercooked meats, unpasteurized cheese, and excess caffeine.
Week 8: Attend your first ultrasound (dating scan) and confirm the heartbeat and due date.
Week 10: Start moisturizing your belly daily to soothe stretching skin and reduce itchiness.
Week 12: Discuss Non-Invasive Prenatal Testing (NIPT) and genetic screening with your healthcare provider.

Second Trimester (Weeks 14-27)

Week 14: Welcome to the "honeymoon phase"! Start shopping for maternity clothes as your bump grows.
Week 16: Start a baby registry and begin researching pediatricians in your local area.
Week 20: Attend your mid-pregnancy anatomy scan (Level II ultrasound). Find out the sex if you wish!
Week 22: Research and sign up for childbirth education classes and lactation consultations.
Week 24: Take your glucose screening test to check for gestational diabetes.
Week 26: Plan your baby shower or "babymoon" before travel becomes too uncomfortable.

Third Trimester (Weeks 28-40+)

Week 28: Start tracking fetal movements. Use a kick counter daily to ensure your baby is active.
Week 30: Finalize your birth plan and discuss your preferences and pain management with your provider.
Week 32: Install the infant car seat and consider having it inspected by a certified technician.
Week 34: Pack your hospital bag for yourself, your partner, and the baby. Keep it near the door.
Week 36: Undergo Group B Strep (GBS) testing. Wash the baby's clothes and finalize the nursery.
Week 38: Know the signs of true labor vs. Braxton Hicks. Familiarize yourself with a contraction timer.
Week 40: Relax, rest, and wait. Try natural ways to induce labor (like walking), but know your baby will arrive when they are ready!
