Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At Pregnancy Week Tracker, we take your privacy seriously. This tool is designed to be completely anonymous and private.

Our site contains links to other websites (like Amazon for product recommendations). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via our contact page.
