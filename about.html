A week calculator you can use without an account

Pregnancy Week Tracker estimates how far along a pregnancy is from the first day of the last period, or from a due date you already have. It then points to that week’s guide and a short list of what people typically do next. No signup. The dates stay in this browser.

How the dates work

The calculator uses the same 280-day (40-week) count from the first day of the last menstrual period that clinics use as a starting estimate — Naegele’s rule, described in ACOG’s methods for estimating the due date. If you enter a due date instead, it counts backward from that date.

An estimated due date is not a deadline. Only a small share of babies arrive on that exact day. An early ultrasound, irregular cycles, or IVF dating can change the number. Your clinician’s date is the one that matters for care.

What the week pages are

Each week page is a general educational summary: typical size comparisons, common notes, and practical prep. Size photos use household objects as a visual aid. They are not ultrasound images and they are not a growth chart for your pregnancy.

Kick counting and contraction timing on this site are stopwatches. They do not diagnose labour, reduced movement, or anything else. If movement changes or you are worried, contact your maternity unit — do not wait on a webpage.

What this is not

  • Not medical advice, not a clinic, not a replacement for prenatal care.
  • Not a login wall, not a pregnancy social network, not an app store download.
  • Not reviewed by a medical board. Public guidelines are cited so you can read the source yourself.

Privacy

Calculator results can be saved in localStorage on your device so a kick counter or week page can remember the last week you ran. Nothing is uploaded to our servers for that. Analytics and ads, if they load, are separate third-party scripts.
