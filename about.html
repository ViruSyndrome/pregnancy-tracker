About Pregnancy Week Tracker

Welcome to Pregnancy Week Tracker — a simple, premium, and privacy-first resource for expectant parents.

Why I Built This Tool

As a father of two, I’ve seen first-hand the incredible journey of pregnancy — and the frustration of trying to find clear, simple information. Most apps today are overloaded with ads, forced sign-ups, and collection of too much personal data.

I wanted to create a free, privacy-first resource for moms and their families. This project is the result of extensive research into fetal development, combined with our unique Scale-Accurate Photography. Unlike generic icons, our comparisons use real-world objects to give you a true sense of your baby's growth.

Scientific Accuracy & Sources

How we calculate your baby's size and progress:

Our week-by-week data, fetal lengths, and development milestones are sourced from established medical and research organizations, including:

  • Carnegie Stages: The global standard for human embryonic development.
  • World Health Organization (WHO): Fetal growth charts and prenatal care guidelines.
  • NIH / PubMed: Clinical research on gestational development and anatomical changes.
  • ACOG (American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists): Standards for gestational age and due date calculation (Naegele's Rule).
