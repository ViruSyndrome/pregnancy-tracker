Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. We Don't Collect Your Data

Pregnancy Week Tracker is a privacy-first tool. All calculations related to your Last Menstrual Period (LMP) or due date happen entirely within your own web browser. We do not store, transmit, or have any access to the dates you enter or the results generated.

2. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand general traffic patterns (e.g., how many people visit the site each day). This data is anonymized and does not include any information that could identify you personally or your specific pregnancy details.

3. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to Amazon (affiliate links). If you click on these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Please refer to Amazon's own privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at hello@pregnancyweektracker.com.
