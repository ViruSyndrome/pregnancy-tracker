How to Choose the Perfect Baby Name (Without Regrets)

Naming a human being is a massive responsibility. From vintage revivals to nature-inspired monikers, here is your guide to picking a name you will love forever.

Top Naming Trends for 2026

This year, parents are looking for meaning, connection to nature, and 'old money' vintage aesthetics. Here are the top trends we are seeing:

Vintage Revival (The 'Grandparent' Names)

  • Eleanor, Hazel, Arthur, Theodore
  • Evelyn, Clara, Silas, Felix

Nature & Botanical

  • Rowan, River, Sage, Wren
  • Willow, Juniper, Brooks, Forest

3 Rules to Avoid "Name Regret"

  1. The "Yell Test": Go to your back door and yell the first and middle name loudly. Does it flow well? Does it sound right?
  2. The Initials Check: Write down the first, middle, and last initials. Make sure they don't spell anything embarrassing (e.g., Benjamin Adams Dad = B.A.D).
  3. The Email Test: Imagine their future professional email address. Does the name combination create any weird hidden words?
