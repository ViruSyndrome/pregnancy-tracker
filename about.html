About Pregnancy Week Tracker

Welcome to Pregnancy Week Tracker, a simple, privacy-first tool designed to help expectant parents track their baby's development without the clutter of traditional apps.

I built this tool for my sister-in-law when she was pregnant. I noticed that most pregnancy apps require forced signups, collect too much personal data, and are often overloaded with ads and marketing emails.

Our Philosophy:

  • Privacy First: We don't store your data. All calculations happen in your browser.
  • No Signups: You don't need an account to use any of our tools.
  • Real Scale: Our "AI Scale View" photos show you exactly how big your baby is compared to a real human hand.

If you have any feedback or suggestions, please feel free to reach out via our contact page.
